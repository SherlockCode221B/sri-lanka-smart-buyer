# Groceries, Fresh Produce, Seafood & Provisions Guide (Sri Lanka)

## 1. Wholesale Agricultural & Fresh Produce Hubs

### A. New Manning Market (Peliyagoda)
- **Specialty**: The national wholesale epicenter for vegetables, greens, and local fruits.
- **Facility**: Relocated from Pettah to a multi-story 13.5-acre modern complex in Peliyagoda. Trucks drive directly up to the upper floors to unload produce coming from Nuwara Eliya, Badulla, Dambulla, Jaffna, and Embilipitiya.
- **Operating Hours**: Active trading begins around **12:00 Midnight and runs until 8:00 AM**.
- **How to Buy**:
  - While built for heavy commercial lorries and retailers, individual consumers and families can buy in sacks or wooden crates (*"pettiya"* or 5–10 kg bundles).
  - Price savings compared to city retail supermarkets (Keells, Cargills, Glomark) range from **40% to 70%** (e.g., carrots, leeks, potatoes, onions, cabbage).
  - Bring a friend, a large tote bag or carton, and wear practical closed-toe shoes.

### B. Peliyagoda Central Fish Market
- **Specialty**: Sri Lanka's largest seafood wholesale auction and distribution center.
- **Catches Available**: Yellowfin tuna (*Kelawalla*), sailfish (*Thalapath*), seer fish (*Thora*), cuttlefish, jumbo prawns, crabs, sprats, and coastal small fish.
- **Operating Hours**: **1:00 AM – 6:30 AM** (by 7:30 AM, primary wholesale auctions are wrapping up and only retail table scraps remain).
- **Buying Strategy**:
  - Buy a whole fish (e.g., 3–6 kg tuna or sailfish) rather than cut steaks. The wholesale auction price per kilogram for a whole fish is often 35–50% cheaper than pre-sliced supermarket portions.
  - Dedicated cutting/cleaning stalls on the perimeter will scale, skin, and slice your whole fish into steaks or curry pieces for a nominal fee (approx. LKR 100–250 total).

### C. Pettah 4th & 5th Cross Streets & Old Moor Street
- **Specialty**: Bulk dry rations, grains, spices, pulses, and dried fish (*Karawala*).
- **Items to Buy Here**:
  - **Rice**: Basmati, Keeri Samba, Nadu, Red Raw (*Rathu Kekulu*) in 10 kg, 25 kg, and 50 kg sacks direct from Polonnaruwa/Ampara mill agents.
  - **Pulses & Spices**: Mysore dhal, whole dried chilies, black pepper, Ceylon cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, cumin seeds.
  - **Provisions**: Sugar, big onions, imported potatoes, dried sprats (*Halmassa*), Maldive fish chips.
- **Savings**: Buying 5–10 kg bags here cuts monthly kitchen pantry expenses by 25–40% compared to branded packaged dry goods.

## 2. Dedicated Economic Centers (DECs) for Outstation Buyers

If you are not located in Colombo, source your bulk produce from the island's regional network of Dedicated Economic Centers:
- **Dambulla DEC (Central Province)**: The biggest vegetable trading crossroads in the country. Produce from northern and upcountry farmers is traded here around the clock. Best for bulk wholesale.
- **Keppetipola DEC (Uva Province)**: Direct farm-gate prices for upcountry vegetables (carrots, leeks, beetroot, beans).
- **Narahenpita DEC (Colombo)**: A more accessible urban economic center with lower prices than supermarkets, though higher than Peliyagoda wholesale.
- **Meegoda & Veyangoda DECs (Western Province)**: Excellent intermediate wholesale distribution points for suburban consumers.

## 3. Supermarket vs Wholesale Strategy: When to Use What

| Channel | Best For | Typical Price Markup | Inspection / Convenience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wholesale Hub (Peliyagoda / Pettah)** | Monthly dry rations, big family events, bulk produce (>5 kg), whole seafood | Baseline (Lowest) | High effort, early morning, cash required, self-transport. |
| **Local Wet Markets / Pola (Weekly Sunday Pola)** | Weekly family vegetables, leafy greens (*kola mita*), fresh coconuts | +15% to +25% | Moderate effort, community vibe, cash bargaining. |
| **Supermarkets (Keells, Cargills, Spar, Glomark)** | Imported specialty foods, dairy, branded packaged sauces, quick top-ups | +35% to +80% | Air-conditioned, card points, cleaned/trimmed, high convenience. |

### Supermarket Deal-Hacks:
- **Loyalty Days**: Cargills (Star Points discounts), Keells (Nexus member 20–25% off selected vegetables on specific weekdays).
- **Bank Card Promotions**: Many commercial banks run 10–20% discounts on fresh produce and meats at leading supermarket chains between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM on weekdays.
- **PickMe Market / Uber Eats Mart**: Watch for 30–50% flash coupons during mid-day off-peak hours for staple goods.
