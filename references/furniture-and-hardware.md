# Furniture, Timber, Automotive Spares & Hardware Guide (Sri Lanka)

## 1. Moratuwa: Timber & Custom Solid Wood Furniture

Moratuwa (southern suburb of Colombo along Galle Road) is Sri Lanka's carpentry capital. You can purchase solid wood dining tables, sofas, beds, wardrobes, and doors at 30–50% less than showroom prices (Damro, Arpico, Singer, Don Carolis).

### The Timber Hierarchy in Sri Lanka:
1. **Teak (*Thekka*)**: The undisputed king of furniture woods. Highly durable, natural water and termite resistance due to rich natural oils, beautiful golden-brown grain with age. Highest value retention.
2. **Mahogany (*Mahogani*)**: Deep reddish-brown hue, fine grain, lighter weight than teak. Excellent for indoor bedroom suites and desks.
3. **Nadun**: Hard, heavy, extremely durable traditional hardwood with dark chocolate streaks. Outstanding for heirloom dining tables.
4. **Jackfruit Wood (*Kos*)**: Naturally bright yellowish-orange, darkens over decades to a rich golden brown. Traditional favorite for doors, doorframes, and temple woodwork.
5. **Mara / Ginisapu**: Budget hardwoods. Good strength, coarser grain. Often used for bed frames and structural sofa framing.

### The "Fake Teak" Scam & How to Beat It:
- **The Scam**: Unscrupulous sellers take cheap softwoods (treated Rubberwood, Acacia/Grandis, or Pinus) and spray them with dark Teak-tinted polyurethane/melamine polish, selling them as "100% Solid Teak".
- **How to Protect Yourself**:
  1. **Demand to Inspect in the "Raw" (*Kalu leeli / Unpolished stage*)**: Never buy a pre-polished, glossy teak item from a roadside vendor unless you are certain of the timber. Visit the workshop and inspect the assembled furniture before the sealer and polish are sprayed. Teak has an unmistakably distinct oily feel, a rich natural grain pattern, and a pleasant, slightly leathery fragrance.
  2. **Look at the End-Grain**: Examine bottom edges, under table leaves, or drawer undersides where polish isn't applied thick.
  3. **Demand Written Warranty Specification**: Ensure the invoice states *"100% Solid Teak wood with 10-year timber warranty"* rather than vague terms like "Hardwood suite".

## 2. Panchikawatte (Colombo 10): Automotive Spare Parts Epicenter

Panchikawatte Road and surrounding lanes near Maradana form Sri Lanka's central hub for automobile and motorcycle spare parts.

### The Spare Part Tiers:
1. **Genuine OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)**:
   - Parts in factory boxes (Toyota Genuine, Suzuki Genuine, Honda Genuine, Bajaj Genuine).
   - *Verification*: Check holographic anti-counterfeit security seals, crisp part-number labels, and clear embossing on the metal/rubber.
2. **High-Grade Japanese/Taiwanese Aftermarket**:
   - Best value for wear-and-tear components (suspension, steering, brakes).
   - **Trusted Brands**: **555 (Sankei)** for ball joints and tie rod ends, **RBI** for rubber bushes and mountings, **KYB (Kayaba)** for shock absorbers, **MK Kashiyama / Akebono** for brake pads, **Denso** for plugs and sensors, **Exedy** for clutch plates.
   - *Price Delta*: Typically 40–60% cheaper than Genuine Agent parts while offering 85–95% of OEM longevity.
3. **Reconditioned / Japanese Imports (*"Japani kali"*)**:
   - Original used components stripped from Japanese domestic market vehicles (half-cuts, doors, headlights, alternators, compressors, steering racks, gearboxes).
   - Sold along Panchikawatte, Darley Road, and Sri Sangaraja Mawatha.
   - *Tip*: Always bring the old/broken part with you to match mounting brackets, pinouts, and dimensions exactly. Request a "1-month checking warranty" on electrical items (alternators, starters, AC compressors).

## 3. Pettah Hardware, Tools & Sanitaryware Hubs

- **Prince Street & China Street**: Power tools, hand tools, drill bits, bearings, water pumps, plumbing fittings.
- **Bankshall Street**: Chemicals, paints, glass, mirror cutting, brass fittings, locks, door handles.
- **Gas Works Street & Bodhiraja Mawatha**: Electrical switches, industrial cables, lighting fixtures, distribution boards.

### Spotting Fake Power Tools (Fake Bosch / Makita / DeWalt):
Pettah is flooded with clone rotary hammers, angle grinders, and cordless drills stamped with fake "BOSCH" or "MAKITA" stickers selling for LKR 6,000–12,000.
- **Signs of a Fake**:
  - The tool body is light and feels hollow; plastic seams are jagged.
  - The power cable is thin, stiff, and lacks rubber insulation.
  - Motor sparks violently through the ventilation slots when triggered.
- **The Smart Alternative**: Instead of buying a fake Bosch for LKR 9,000 that burns out in 2 months, buy a genuine budget industrial brand such as **DongCheng, Total, Ingco, or DCA**. These offer legitimate warranties, metal gear casings, and easily accessible replacement carbon brushes at wholesale prices.
