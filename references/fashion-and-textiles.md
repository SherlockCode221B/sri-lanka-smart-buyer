# Fashion, Garments & Textiles Sourcing Guide (Sri Lanka)

## 1. Physical Wholesale Garment & Fabric Hubs

### A. Pamunuwa / "Redda Kadamandiya" (Maharagama)
- **Specialty**: The historic epicentre of budget garments, fabrics, and apparel manufacturing surplus in Sri Lanka.
- **What You Find**:
  - **Export Surplus & Factory Overruns**: High-quality cotton t-shirts, activewear, hoodies, dresses, and children's clothing manufactured for global brands (Nike, Gap, Ralph Lauren, Victoria's Secret, Decathlon) in Sri Lankan export apparel plants (MAS Holdings, Brandix, Hirdaramani) that have excess inventory or minor defects.
  - **Ready-Made Domestic Wear**: Casual shirts, cotton trousers, shorts, nightwear, frocks, school uniforms.
  - **Fabric by the Yard / Kilo**: Linen, raw cotton, jersey knit, chiffon, silk, denim, and curtain materials sold either by length or weighed in kilograms (*"Kilo redda"*).
- **Timing & Logistics**:
  - **Best Days**: Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday.
  - **Best Hours**: **6:00 AM – 9:00 AM**. The wholesale bundle trucks unload at dawn. By 10:00 AM, the narrow lanes are jammed with crowds and the best pieces have been picked clean by boutique resellers.

### B. Pettah 2nd Cross Street, 3rd Cross Street & Prince Street (Colombo 11)
- **Specialty**: Saree fabrics, wedding wear materials, bridal lace, suiting fabrics, buttons, zippers, tailor accessories, and traditional ethnic wear.
- **Best For**: Buying bulk fabrics for custom tailoring or event outfits. Prices for high-grade linen and cotton suiting are 40–60% cheaper than department store fabric counters.

### C. Malwatta Road (Pettah, Colombo 11 - Opposite Fort Railway Station)
- **Specialty**: Footwear, sneakers, slippers, formal leather shoes, backpacks, travel duffel bags, suitcases.
- **Tip**: Shoes here range from cheap locally molded rubber slippers (LKR 300–600) to imported sneakers. Carefully flex the sole and check glue joints at the toe cap—low-grade batches come unglued after the first rain.

### D. Maharagama High Street
- Beyond Pamunuwa lane, the main high street features large competitive multi-story clothing retailers: **Thilakawardhana Textiles, CIB Shopping Center, Spring & Summer, Nolimit, Cool Planet**.
- Excellent for buyers who want air-conditioned comfort, fitting rooms, and verified quality at prices 20–30% lower than prime Colombo malls (Colombo City Centre or One Galle Face).

## 2. Quality Inspection & Spotting "Good Surplus" vs "Damaged Junk"

Export overruns and factory rejects in Sri Lanka are categorised into three grades:

| Grade | What it Means | How to Identify | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade A (Clean Overrun)** | Perfect excess production. The factory made 10,000 units, the buyer took 9,500; the remaining 500 are liquidated. | Tags are often snipped or marked with a black felt marker to prevent retail returns. No visible flaws. | **Instant Buy** (Best value in LK). |
| **Grade B (Cosmetic Reject)** | Minor sewing anomaly or printing imperfection. | A skipped stitch under the armpit, slightly misaligned pocket, or slight dye variance. | **Great Buy** if the flaw is unnoticeable or easily stitched. |
| **Grade C (Structural Defect)** | Fabric run, warped collar, asymmetrical armholes, or holes. | Lay the shirt flat: check if the side seams twist around the torso (*"spiral seam defect"*). | **Avoid**; will warp or unravel after one wash. |

### Practical In-Hand Inspection Routine:
1. **The Seam Tug Test**: Gently pull the side seams and shoulder seams. If threads snap or loosen, the tension was bad during stitching.
2. **The Collar Check**: Stretch the neck opening and release. Good rib-knit collars snap back immediately. If it stays stretched like a wave, it will sag after the first wash.
3. **Turn It Inside Out**: Never judge a garment from the outside. Look at the overlock stitching inside. Clean, uniform threads without loose bunches indicate a genuine export garment.

## 3. Fair Price Benchmarks (LKR)

*Note: Prices reflect standard street wholesale and value retail rates.*

- **Plain Cotton Crew-Neck T-Shirts (Export Surplus)**: LKR 600 – 1,200 (vs LKR 3,500–6,000 in shopping malls).
- **Polo Shirts (Cotton/Pique)**: LKR 1,200 – 2,200.
- **Pure Linen Shirts (Long-Sleeve / Short-Sleeve)**: LKR 1,800 – 3,200 (vs LKR 7,000–12,000 branded).
- **Denim Jeans (Sturdy cotton stretch)**: LKR 1,800 – 3,500.
- **Children's Everyday Outfits (Frocks, shorts, tees)**: LKR 350 – 900.
- **Linen / Cotton Fabric (per yard)**: LKR 450 – 900.
- **Curtain Fabric (per yard)**: LKR 350 – 850.
- **Sneakers / Casual Shoes (Malwatta Rd)**: LKR 1,800 – 4,500.
