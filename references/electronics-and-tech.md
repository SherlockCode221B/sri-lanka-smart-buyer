# Electronics, Smartphones & Tech Hardware Sourcing Guide (Sri Lanka)

## 1. Physical Wholesale & Retail Tech Hubs

### A. Pettah First Cross Street (Colombo 11)
- **Specialty**: Mobile accessories, wholesale cables, chargers, power banks, replacement screens, phone repair tools, small audio devices, CCTV cameras.
- **Vibe & Structure**: Intense, high-density street market. Ground-floor and second-floor stalls (e.g., inside narrow shopping passages).
- **Best For**: Buying accessories in multi-packs (e.g., 3-5 tempered glasses at LKR 150-250 each vs LKR 1,000 at a retail mall; fast-charging cables for LKR 400-800).
- **Watch Out**: Massive prevalence of "first copy" fake Samsung/Apple cables, clone JBL Bluetooth speakers, and dangerous uncertified fast-chargers. Always insist on reputable budget brands like **Baseus, Remax, Joyroom, Havit, or Ugreen**.

### B. Unity Plaza (Bambalapitiya, Colombo 04)
- **Specialty**: Sri Lanka's undisputed IT and PC hardware headquarters. Laptops, custom desktop components, monitors, networking gear, printers, storage.
- **Top Shops for Enthusiasts & Budget Value**:
  - **Barclays Computers**: Authorized distributor, long-standing warranty reliability for HP, Dell, Asus, Acer.
  - **Nanotek Computer Solutions**: Renowned for custom gaming rigs, PC parts (CPUs, GPUs, RAM, motherboards), competitive margins, and straightforward warranty claims.
  - **Redline Technologies**: High-end workstations and gaming rigs, meticulous assembly.
  - **TecRoot**: Handheld consoles (Steam Deck, ROG Ally, Switch), console games, and audiophile gear.
  - **Tech Zone, Winsoft, Access Computers**: Competitive for peripherals and storage.
- **Strategy**: Unity Plaza houses dozens of shops across 5+ floors. Never buy from the first shop you enter. Walk up the floors, collect printed quote slips (*"Quotation bill"*), and ask competing vendors to match or beat the cash price.

### C. Liberty Plaza (Kollupitiya) & Majestic City (Bambalapitiya)
- **Specialty**: Smartphones (new and used), Apple products, tablets, smartwatches, and camera gear.
- **Reputable Independent Mobile Sellers**: Doctor Mobile, Apple Asia, Greenware, Thariq Computers, Cellular Arcade.
- **Key Advantage**: Good spot to find "Open Box", "Used / Grade A", and international version devices at a 15–30% discount compared to authorized brand flagship stores.

## 2. Regulatory & Quality Verification: Mandatory Steps

### The TRCSL IMEI Verification (Crucial for Phones)
In Sri Lanka, the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRCSL) restricts unapproved mobile imports. If an IMEI is not registered, the device may be blacklisted from local cellular networks (Dialog, Mobitel, SLT, Airtel, Hutch).
1. Dial `*#06#` on the phone's keypad to display the 15-digit IMEI (check both IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 for dual-SIM).
2. Send an SMS to **1909** with the text:
   ```text
   IMEI [15-digit IMEI number]
   Example: IMEI 354892091234567
   ```
3. Wait for the confirmation reply confirming device registration status.
4. *Rule*: If the seller refuses to let you test the IMEI or claims "it doesn't matter for non-contract phones", walk away.

### Warranty Decoding in Sri Lanka
Always confirm the exact warranty category before paying:
1. **Authorized Agent / Company Warranty** (Gold Standard):
   - Backed by official national distributors (Singer, Abans, Softlogic, John Keells, Metropolitan, DIMO).
   - Higher price (15-25% more), but full replacement/service parts and valid TRCSL registration guaranteed.
2. **Shop Warranty** (Mid-Tier / Grey Market):
   - Backed solely by that specific shop in Pettah or Liberty Plaza.
   - Usually 1-year service warranty, but *hardware parts* may only be covered for 1 to 3 months. If the shop closes, the warranty is worthless.
3. **Checking Warranty** (Budget / Second-Hand):
   - Valid for only 3 to 7 days.
   - You must stress-test battery, screen, cameras, cellular connectivity, and charging immediately upon getting home.

## 3. Digital Channels & Online Hacks

### Daraz LK (daraz.lk)
- **Daraz Mall**: Look for the red "Mall" badge for guaranteed genuine electronics.
- **Discount Stacking Routine**:
  1. **Bank Card Discounts**: Check the weekly schedule (e.g., Commercial Bank on Monday, Sampath on Tuesday, HNB on Wednesday, Amex on Thursday). These give an automatic 10–15% instant discount at checkout.
  2. **Collectable Vouchers**: Collect store vouchers + Daraz platform vouchers + free shipping vouchers before adding to cart.
  3. **Daraz Coins**: Check in daily on the mobile app to redeem coins for an extra 2–5% off.
  4. **Mega Sale Days**: Wait for 11.11 (November), 12.12 (December), Sinhala-Tamil New Year (April), or Monthly PayDay sales (around the 25th–30th).

### ikman.lk & Facebook Tech Groups (Used Deals)
- Best for used graphics cards, RAM, laptops, and secondhand phones.
- **Top FB Groups**: "PC Gamers of Sri Lanka - Marketplace", "Sri Lanka Tech Enthusiasts", "Apple Buy & Sell Sri Lanka".
- **Inspection Protocol**: Never buy a laptop or phone in a street corner. Meet at a location with power outlets and Wi-Fi (e.g., a café or Keells lounge) so you can run:
  - Battery health report (`powercfg /batteryreport` on Windows).
  - CrystalDiskInfo (to check SSD/HDD health and power-on hours).
  - Benchmark stress test (FurMark / Cinebench) to ensure the GPU/CPU does not thermal throttle or crash.
