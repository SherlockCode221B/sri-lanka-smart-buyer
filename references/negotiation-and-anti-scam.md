# Negotiation Tactics, Language Phrases & Anti-Scam Playbook (Sri Lanka)

## 1. Local Negotiation Playbook & Cultural Dynamics

Bargaining in Sri Lanka is an expected cultural practice in open street markets (Pettah, Pamunuwa, Panchikawatte, local polas, and independent electronics stores in Liberty/Unity Plaza). It is NOT practiced in fixed-price supermarkets, authorized brand flagships (Singer, Abans, Odel), or branded malls.

### The Cash Advantage
- **The Golden Rule**: Always carry crisp Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) cash.
- **Why**: Street vendors in Pettah and Panchikawatte pay transaction processing fees on credit card machines (often 2.5%–3.5%) or want to keep transactions off formal card books.
- **The Tactic**: Agree on a tentative price, then say: *"Cash walin danma gannawanam kiyak adu wenawada?"* (If I pay right now in cash, how much will you knock off?). This routinely unlocks an extra 5% to 12% discount.

## 2. Practical Bargaining Scripts (Sinhala & Tamil)

### In Sinhala:
1. **"Meyeta wada adu karanna barida?"**
   - *Phonetics*: *May-yet-uh wah-dah ah-doo kahr-ahn-nuh bah-ri-dah?*
   - *Meaning*: "Can't you reduce the price lower than this?" (Polite, universal opening).
2. **"Wholesale ganata denna puluwanda?"**
   - *Phonetics*: *Whole-sale gah-nah-tuh den-nah poo-loo-wahn-dah?*
   - *Meaning*: "Can you give this to me at the wholesale rate?" (Use when buying 2+ items or bundles).
3. **"Adu karala anthima gana kiyanna."**
   - *Phonetics*: *Ah-doo kahr-ah-lah ahn-thee-muh gah-nuh kee-yahn-nuh.*
   - *Meaning*: "Cut the fluff and tell me your final bottom price." (Direct, used after initial back-and-forth).
4. **"Kollo, thawa tikak adu karala denna."**
   - *Phonetics*: *Kol-lo, thah-wah thee-kahk ah-doo kahr-ah-lah den-nah.*
   - *Meaning*: "Brother/Buddy, reduce it just a bit more for me." (Colloquial, friendly rapport builder for young vendors in Pettah or Pamunuwa).
5. **"Checking warranty ekak thiyanawada? Bill eka denna."**
   - *Phonetics*: *Check-ing wahr-ahn-tee eh-kahk thee-yah-nah-wah-dah? Bill eh-kuh den-nah.*
   - *Meaning*: "Is there a checking warranty? Please issue the printed bill." (Essential for tech/hardware).

### In Tamil:
1. **"Konjam koraichu thara mudiyuma?"**
   - *Phonetics*: *Kohn-juhm koh-ry-choo thah-ruh moo-dee-yoo-mah?*
   - *Meaning*: "Can you reduce it a little bit?" (Polite opening).
2. **"Wholesale vilaiyil thara mudiyuma?"**
   - *Phonetics*: *Whole-sale vi-lai-yil thah-ruh moo-dee-yoo-mah?*
   - *Meaning*: "Can you give this at wholesale price?"
3. **"Kadaisi vilai enna?"**
   - *Phonetics*: *Kah-dai-si vi-lai ehn-nah?*
   - *Meaning*: "What is your last/final price?"

## 3. Online Classifieds & Second-Hand Safety (ikman.lk & Facebook Groups)

Second-hand shopping on ikman.lk or Facebook Marketplace offers great deals on laptops, smartphones, furniture, and vehicles, but is a prime hunting ground for scammers.

### The 4 Unbreakable Safety Rules:
1. **The Zero-Advance Rule**:
   - **NEVER** transfer money via eZ Cash, mCash, or direct bank transfer as a "token advance", "holding deposit", or "courier shipping fee".
   - *The Common Scam*: The seller posts an iPhone or GPU at a mouth-watering price (e.g., RTX 3070 for LKR 65,000). When you message, they claim they live in Jaffna, Anuradhapura, or Galle, but can courier it if you transfer an advance of LKR 2,000–5,000. As soon as you transfer, their phone number is disconnected and the profile is deleted.
2. **Safe Inspection Locations**:
   - Never meet in secluded residential alleys or isolated roads.
   - Ideal meeting spots: The seating area of a prominent supermarket (Keells / Cargills Food City), a major shopping mall lobby (Majestic City, Liberty Plaza, One Galle Face), or a well-lit coffee shop with power sockets to test electricals.
3. **Vehicle & High-Value Inspection**:
   - For used cars or motorbikes found on ikman, always pay for an independent pre-purchase inspection at a reputable vehicle testing center (e.g., Carepoint, Auto Miraj, Laughs, or a trusted local mechanic) before making any down payment. Check the CR (Certificate of Registration) against the chassis and engine number.
4. **Hardware Stress-Testing Before Handover**:
   - For phones: Dial `*#06#` and verify the IMEI via SMS to **1909** (TRCSL). Test charging port, microphone, speaker, all cameras, and Wi-Fi.
   - For laptops: Run `powercfg /batteryreport` in command prompt to check battery capacity loss and cycle count.
